  Use this skill when a component or screen should face worst-case data before it ships: very long
  or unbreakable names and emails, one-letter names, missing fields, zero or huge counts, long
  labels, non-Latin text, emoji and extreme numbers, reported with what broke and the fix for each.

  working through a component's states (ui-states) or design critique (ui-critique).
