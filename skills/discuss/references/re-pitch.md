# Re-pitch an explanation

> Internal reference selected by `discuss` when the operator says the explanation did not land.
> Adapted from Matt Pocock’s MIT-licensed `wait-what` skill.

Stop advancing the argument. Re-state the necessary context, use short sentences and ASD-STE100-style
plain English, and use the repository’s established domain language. Explain the current state, why
it matters, and the one next distinction or choice. Do not repeat the previous wording or add a plan.
