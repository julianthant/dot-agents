# Humanizing and fidelity

Use for substantial rewrites and explicit requests to humanize text. Apply the entrypoint's humanizing
pass to every draft; this reference adds a closer inspection when the text warrants it.

Read a supplied voice sample before editing. Notice sentence length, contractions, paragraph openings,
punctuation, technical vocabulary and the writer's degree of warmth. Keep intentional habits while
fixing distracting repetition. With no sample, choose a natural register appropriate to the audience.

First separate content from presentation. Identify the claims, evidence, qualifications, commitments
and exact quotations that must survive. This is a working check, not a required separate document.
Then reorganize the draft around the reader's question or action and revise sentences in that order.

Watch for patterns rather than isolated forbidden words:

- Claims of significance without an explanation; elaborate phrases that say no more than a simple verb.
- Attributing statements to unnamed experts; adding plausible detail to hide a missing source.
- Repeating the same opening or formula, renaming a subject repeatedly, forcing every point into a trio.
- Excessive emphasis, headings or decorative punctuation; paragraphs that repeat an introduction or
  conclude with generic optimism instead of a useful fact or action.
- A voice that is uniformly polished but unlike the supplied sample. Restore supported quirks and
  natural rhythm without inventing a personal story, emotion or opinion.

Example, supplied facts: eight respondents; six reported saving time; duration was not measured.
A faithful sentence is: "Six of the eight respondents said they saved time. We did not measure how
much." "The pilot improved efficiency by 75%" changes the denominator and invents an effect size.
Similarly, "We aim to launch on October 6, pending review" must not become "We launch October 6."

After revision, compare source and output claim by claim. Preserve relevant dates, quantities, causal
limits, attribution and modality. If an unsupported assertion matters to the message, flag it or
retain it as an attributed assertion; do not silently certify it. A style edit is not a fact check.
Verify new factual claims through the applicable research workflow when the task requires them.
